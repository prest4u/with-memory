# Introduction

With. is memory that stays on the machine you already own.

You do not log in. You do not upload a corpus. You do not replace your editor with another product. You keep the tools you have. They share one store.

## Why it exists

AI harnesses forget between sessions, or they remember in a format you do not own. Vendor graphs, hosted indexes, and “memory apps” all ask you to move the truth somewhere else.

With. keeps the truth in a SQLite file you can copy, back up, or delete. The vault is a projection. If Obsidian is closed, the facts are still there.

## How a day looks

1. Something changes: a decision, a path, a name.
2. The harness searches first. Expired rows are history, not advice.
3. It writes one to three sentences: what, where, status, next door.
4. If the new sentence contradicts an old one, the old one is deprecated. It is not rewritten in place.
5. `sync` rebuilds the human-facing notes.

That loop is the product. Everything else is plumbing.

## What the name means

*With* is lowercase on purpose. The period is the mark, not punctuation. The software is meant to accompany work, leave a trail, and be ready for the next step.

The command is still `eric-memory`. Renaming it would break installs that already work.
